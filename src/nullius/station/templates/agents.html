{# The eleven actors. One build per Role, so somebody at a workstation is identifiable without reading a label: the Skeptic is hooded and carries a glass, the Custodian is sealed into a suit with a visor slot and a keyring, the Replicator wears a blindfold band and drags a ghost of itself, the Builder is in a hard hat with a tool belt. SYSTEM is a servitor and not a person, because it is the deterministic control plane — it compiles the spec, runs the sandbox and writes the events — and giving it a face would be the drawing making a claim the architecture does not. M28 gave them volume. Until then a figure was flat colour: one hue for the coat, one dark for everything else, and past the second click of zoom it read as a paper cut-out. Everything below is built from the same five parts — boots with soles, legs where the far one sits in shadow, a torso with a lit edge and a shaded side, sleeved arms ending in gloved hands, and a face with a brow — plus the one piece of kit that belongs to that role and nobody else. Each also wears an insignia, which is the only thing on the figure that differs by construction rather than by draughtsmanship.
